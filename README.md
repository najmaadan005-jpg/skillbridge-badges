# Skillbridge Badges

Build a web app called SkillProof that helps refugees prove their skills when they have no paper credentials.

Pages:

1. Landing page: a short problem statement ("Skilled refugees are ignored because their certificates are missing") and Get Started / Employer Login buttons.

2. Sign up and profile: name, country of origin, profession, years of experience.

3. Skill test: the user picks a skill (web development, bookkeeping, nursing basics, customer service) and answers 10 practical multiple-choice questions with a progress bar.

4. Result and badge: show the score, and if it is 70% or higher, generate a verified badge with a public shareable link.

5. Employer dashboard: browse and search verified candidates by skill and score, with a Contact button.

Design: clean, modern, trustworthy, with a blue and green color scheme. It must work on mobile.

Mention the UN SDGs 4 and 8 in the footer.
